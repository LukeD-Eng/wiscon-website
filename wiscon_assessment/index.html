What the audit covers

Five places where WhatsApp workflows lose their record.

  • 1Quote acceptance — scope, price, and terms confirmed before you mobilise
  • 2Variation approvals — extras confirmed before your team lifts a finger
  • 3Snag instructions — defects issued, fixed, photographed, and signed off
  • 4Lead qualification — budget, scope, timing, and photos captured before you waste a site visit
  • 5Site admin records — the daily notes, payment milestones, and evidence gaps that become disputes later

What changes

When the workflow has a record.

  • Get quote acceptance and variation approvals confirmed before work starts, so the job follows what was agreed — not what someone remembers later.
  • Filter serious leads from tyre-kickers before you spend time calling, driving, or quoting with missing information.
  • Track snags from photo to subcontractor dispatch to completion proof, so "I sorted it" becomes a record instead of an argument.
